SDLC Origin vs. Detection Mapping:
Origin Phase: The fundamental bugs (like Callendar Square) originated in the Implementation and Testing phases.
Detection Phase: These bugs were only formally acknowledged and legally detected during the Operations/Maintenance phase, decades after rollout.
Cost-of-Late-Detection Argument:
A synchronization bug caught during the Testing phase would have cost developers hours to fix.
Because it was pushed into the Operations phase and legally weaponized, the cost of late detection ballooned to over £1 billion in compensation, immense reputational destruction, and severe human tragedy.
