Preventive Controls
Independent Technical Audits: Conduct regular external reviews to check the accuracy, reliability, and known defects of high-risk financial systems, providing an outside check against internal bias.
Strong Defect Escalation Process: Implement a strict workflow (Report → Investigate → Assess Risk → Escalate → Fix → Retest → Monitor) to ensure serious problems reach decision-makers before affecting more users.
Independent Verification Before Legal Action: Mandate that system-generated data is independently checked and verified before any serious disciplinary or legal action is taken against a user.
Protected Whistleblowing: Establish a safe, protected channel for engineers and users to report serious technical or organizational problems outside of their normal management chain.
Continuous Monitoring and User Feedback: Analyze repeated complaints collectively to identify system-wide patterns rather than treating them as isolated, unrelated incidents.
